Features

Everything around your evening film

From picking a film to looking back on your year, without spreadsheets and without handing your history to anyone.

Tonight in three taps

Who is watching, how much time you have and what you are in the mood for. Filmlog picks three unseen films from your watchlist or Plex library and respects the age limit of your companions.

A diary that fills itself

Finish a film in Plex and it appears in your diary. Choose which Plex Home profiles count. The nightly sync marks every film on your server.

Bring your Trakt history

Upload your Trakt export once: viewings, ratings, watchlist and lists arrive in order. Run it again whenever you like, nothing is duplicated.

Lists you can actually sort

Drag films into the order you want to see them. Filter by your rating, TMDB rating, genre, decade, runtime and age rating, all kept in the address bar.

Film pages that answer everything

Trailer, cast, the whole collection with your status, every streaming offer in Germany and which of your own subscriptions carries the film.

Your year in films

Hours watched, top genres, favourite directors, where you disagree with TMDB and which series you never finished. Plus the German box office charts since 1970.

How it works

Up and running in one evening

  1. 1

    Deploy one image

    One Docker image runs the app, the queue worker and the scheduler next to PostgreSQL. A Coolify compose file and a GitHub Actions pipeline are included.

  2. 2

    Bring your history

    Add your TMDB_TOKEN, upload a Trakt export and point a Plex webhook at Filmlog. Every part is optional.

  3. 3

    Ask it tonight

    Open Filmlog on your phone, answer three questions and press Läuft gerade. Rate it when the credits roll.

Your data

Your viewing history belongs to you

Filmlog runs where you run it. There is no Filmlog account, no cloud service and no analytics. What you watched never leaves your server.

  • Login with a passkey, a one time login link as fallback and optional authenticator codes
  • Exactly one account, no registration, no passwords
  • Secrets only in your environment, never in the code
  • Film data from TMDB, your own history stays in your PostgreSQL
FAQ

Questions before you set it up

Is Filmlog made for several people?

No. Filmlog has exactly one account, set by FILMLOG_ADMIN_EMAIL. Companions such as family members are recorded per viewing, and they can carry an age limit for the suggestions.

Do I need Plex or Trakt?

No. Both are optional. Without them you log viewings from the film page or with Läuft gerade on the start page. The Plex webhook needs Plex Pass.

Where does the film data come from?

From TMDB. You need a free TMDB account and an API Read Access Token. Streaming offers for Germany come from JustWatch through TMDB, the cinema charts from InsideKino.

What language is the app in?

The interface is German. Film data is fetched in German for the region Germany; language and region can be changed in the configuration.

What do I need to run it?

A server with Docker, PostgreSQL 17 and an SMTP account for login links. The image is built for PHP 8.5 with Laravel 13 and needs no Redis.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes, Filmlog is designed for the phone first, with a bottom navigation and dialogs that open as sheets. It works just as well on a desktop browser.

Can I import from other services?

Today Filmlog imports Trakt exports and receives Plex scrobbles. Other sources are not supported yet.
